REUSABLE COMPONENTS

`FadeIn`
- Motion wrapper using `whileInView`.
- `viewport={{ once: true, margin: '-50px', amount: 0.15 }}`.
- Accept delay, duration, x, and y.
- Easing around `[0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1]`.

`Magnet`
- Mouse-following magnetic hover effect.
- Use pointer distance from element center and a low movement strength.

- Character-level opacity reveal driven by scroll progress.
- Preserve spaces and punctuation.

`GradientButton`

`OutlineButton`

`ProjectModal`
